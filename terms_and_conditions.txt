BUSINESS PROFILE — TERMS & CONDITIONS / CONTENT RULES
=======================================================

These rules govern what a generated or user-edited business profile summary
may contain. The copywriter agent must follow them when producing text, and
the compliance agent must check the final text against them. Flagged items
are reviewed by a human team before publication.

Edit this file to change the rules — the app reads it at runtime, so no code
changes are needed.

-------------------------------------------------------
1. LENGTH
-------------------------------------------------------
1.1 The profile summary must not exceed 500 characters, including spaces and
    punctuation.
1.2 Text that exceeds 500 characters must be flagged as a violation.

-------------------------------------------------------
2. TRUTHFULNESS & CLAIMS
-------------------------------------------------------
2.1 No false, misleading, or unverifiable claims about the business, its
    products, or its services.
2.2 No guarantees that cannot be substantiated (e.g. "guaranteed results",
    "100% success", "risk-free") unless clearly supportable.
2.3 No unsupported superlatives or "best/only/number one" style claims
    (e.g. "the best in the country", "the only provider") without evidence.
2.4 No fabricated statistics, awards, certifications, ratings, or endorsements.
2.5 No claims of partnerships, affiliations, or accreditations that are not
    genuine.

-------------------------------------------------------
3. FAIR & LAWFUL CONTENT
-------------------------------------------------------
3.1 No discriminatory, offensive, or exclusionary language regarding race,
    gender, religion, nationality, disability, age, or sexual orientation.
3.2 No disparagement of named competitors.
3.3 No content that promotes illegal activity or unsafe practices.
3.4 No hate speech, harassment, or threatening language.

-------------------------------------------------------
4. REGULATED & SENSITIVE CLAIMS
-------------------------------------------------------
4.1 No medical, health, legal, or financial advice or outcome promises
    (e.g. "cures", "guaranteed returns", "will win your case").
4.2 No pricing or financial terms presented as binding offers.
4.3 Claims in regulated industries must be framed as general descriptions,
    not professional advice.

-------------------------------------------------------
5. PRIVACY & DATA
-------------------------------------------------------
5.1 No personal data of identifiable private individuals (names, contact
    details, ID numbers) beyond the business's own public contact info.
5.2 No confidential client information.

-------------------------------------------------------
6. TONE & PROFESSIONALISM
-------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Language must be professional and suitable for a public business profile.
6.2 No profanity, slurs, or explicit content.
6.3 No placeholder or robotic phrasing (e.g. "insert value here", "[TODO]").

-------------------------------------------------------
NOTES FOR REVIEWERS
-------------------------------------------------------
- The compliance agent flags potential issues only; it is not a legal
  authority. A human team makes the final decision on each flagged item.
- When a rule is violated, the flag should quote the exact sentence or phrase
  from the profile that caused it, so reviewers can locate it precisely.
